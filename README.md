# 🎓 Freshers Career Copilot

> An AI-powered career support platform designed to help college students build career readiness through personalized guidance, daily progress tracking, and AI-assisted learning.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://freshers-career-copilot.onrender.com/

---

## 📌 Overview

Freshers Career Copilot is an AI-powered student career support platform that brings multiple career-preparation tools together in one place.

Many students know that they need to prepare for internships and placements but are unsure about where to start, what skills to learn, or how to track their progress.

Freshers Career Copilot provides a structured workflow that helps students assess their readiness, track daily preparation, receive personalized AI guidance, and solve learning doubts.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Career Readiness Analyzer

Helps students understand their current level of career preparedness by evaluating important areas such as skills, learning progress, and career goals.

### 📅 Daily Progress Tracker

Allows students to maintain a consistent preparation routine and monitor their progress over time.

### 🤖 AI Mentor Guidance

Provides personalized AI-based guidance to help students identify areas for improvement and decide what to focus on next.

### 💬 AI Doubt Solver

Allows students to ask learning-related questions and receive AI-assisted explanations.

### 📈 Progress Dashboard

Provides a centralized view of career-preparation activities and progress.

### 🎯 Personalized Career Support

Connects assessment, daily tracking, AI guidance, and doubt solving into one student-focused platform.
